Our Core Purpose

About
Amana
Capital.

Forging clarity, discipline, and long-horizon conviction in East African capital markets — for every Tanzanian investor, at home and across the diaspora.

Amana Capital East Africa Limited exists to deliver institutional-grade investment research, advisory, and fund management services rooted in the Dar es Salaam Stock Exchange and East African capital markets.

Our core purpose is to equip Tanzanian investors — both at home and across the global diaspora — with the clarity, discipline, and long-horizon perspective needed to build lasting wealth in the region's frontier markets.

Heritage

Our Story & Founding Principle

"Amana" — the Swahili and Arabic word for trust, honesty, and integrity. It is the foundation of everything we do.

Founded on the belief that East African markets possess deep, undiscovered value, we set out to build an institution capable of uncovering it — and making that research accessible to every Tanzanian investor, regardless of their starting point or location.

We bring institutional-grade research and disciplined investment management to the Dar es Salaam Stock Exchange, closing the information gap that has for too long prevented the region's most compelling opportunities from being properly recognised by the investors who deserve to benefit from them.
