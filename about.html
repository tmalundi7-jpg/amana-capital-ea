About Us

Amana Capital East Africa Limited exists to deliver institutional-grade investment research, advisory, and fund management services rooted in the Dar es Salaam Stock Exchange and East African capital markets. Its core purpose is to equip Tanzanian investors — both at home and across the global diaspora — with the clarity, discipline, and long-horizon perspective needed to build lasting wealth in the region's frontier markets.

Our Story & Principle

"Amana" translates to trust. It is the fundamental operating principle that guides every decision we make. Founded on the belief that East African markets possess deep, undiscovered value, we set out to build an institution capable of uncovering it.

We bring institutional-grade research and disciplined investment management to the Dar es Salaam Stock Exchange, closing the information gap and unlocking genuine value.

Board of Directors

Amana Capital East Africa is guided by a Board of Directors committed to the highest standards of corporate governance, transparency, and fiduciary duty. The Board oversees strategic direction, risk management, and regulatory compliance to ensure alignment with the long-term interests of our stakeholders.

Board composition and independent non-executive appointments will be announced upon finalization of our CMSA registration process.

Environmental, Social, & Governance (ESG)

We believe that sustainable investing is critical to long-term wealth creation. Our ESG framework is integrated directly into our fundamental analysis process for all DSE-listed equities and corporate debt issuances.

  • Environmental Stewardship: Assessing the climate resilience and resource efficiency of portfolio companies.
  • Social Responsibility: Ensuring fair labor practices, community engagement, and stakeholder alignment.
  • Governance Excellence: Demanding board independence, executive accountability, and minority shareholder protection.

Regulatory Status

Amana Capital East Africa Limited is a registered investment advisory and fund management firm regulated by the Capital Markets and Securities Authority (CMSA), Tanzania. Stockbroking licence pending.
