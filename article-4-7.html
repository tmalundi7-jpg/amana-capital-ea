Article 4.7 - Putting It All Together: A Step-by-Step Stock Analysis Checklist

By Amana Capital East Africa – Investor Education Series

You now have all the pieces: macroeconomics, microeconomics, financial statements, competitive analysis, valuation, and technical context. This checklist is your repeatable process.

The Amana Capital EA Stock Analysis Checklist

  • Macro Context: Where in the economic cycle? BOT rate stance? Inflation? Exchange rate?
  • Industry Analysis: Is the sector growing? Cyclical or defensive? Key drivers and risks?
  • Business Model: How does it make money? Competitive moat?
  • Financial Health: Revenue and profit growing? Margins stable? Debt manageable? Free cash flow positive?
  • Management Quality: Transparent? Wise capital allocation? Sustainable dividend?
  • Valuation: P/E, P/B, dividend yield reasonable versus peers and history? Undervalued or overvalued?
  • Technical Snapshot: Uptrend, downtrend, consolidation? Support/resistance? Volume confirming?
  • Decision: Based on the above, buy, hold, or sell? Set a target price and a stop-loss if used.

Further Reading: Move to portfolio construction with Risk and Return: The Only Free Lunch Is Diversification.

Action Step: Print this checklist. The next time you consider a trade on the DSE, complete all eight steps before acting.
