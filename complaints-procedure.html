Complaints Procedure

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Amana Capital EA Ltd takes complaints seriously. If you are unhappy with our website, our research content, or how we have handled your data, we want to hear from you so we can put things right.

Regulatory note: our registration with the Capital Markets and Securities Authority (CMSA) of Tanzania is currently pending. We operate as an independent research publisher, not a licensed advisory firm. The procedure below is our own internal process.

1. How to complain

Email us at info@amana-capital-ea.co.tz with:

  • Your name and contact details.
  • A clear description of the problem.
  • When it happened, and any relevant page or reference (e.g. article title and date).
  • What you would like us to do about it.

2. What happens next

  1. Acknowledgement: we will confirm receipt of your complaint within 5 business days.
  2. Investigation: a member of our management team will review the matter fairly and impartially.
  3. Resolution: we aim to give you a full response within 30 days of receiving your complaint. If we need longer, we will tell you why and when to expect a reply.

3. If you are not satisfied

If our response does not resolve the matter, you may ask for your complaint to be reviewed by a different member of our management team. Email info@amana-capital-ea.co.tz with "Complaint review request" in the subject line.

Once our CMSA registration is complete, complaints relating to regulated activities may also be directed to the Capital Markets and Securities Authority of Tanzania. We will update this page with the relevant CMSA contact details at that time.

4. Keeping records

We keep a record of all complaints and how they were resolved, in line with good practice. Complaint records are handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy.
