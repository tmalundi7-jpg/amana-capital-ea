Cookie Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Amana Capital EA Ltd ("we", "us") uses cookies on amana-capital-ea.co.tz. This policy explains what cookies are, which ones we use, and how you can control them.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences and understand how visitors use it. Most cookies do not identify you personally.

2. Cookies we use

Essential cookies

Required for the site to work (e.g. remembering your cookie-consent choice). These cannot be switched off. They do not track you.

Preference cookies

Remember choices you make, such as display settings. These are optional.

Analytics cookies

Help us understand how visitors use the site (e.g. which pages are popular). Data is aggregated and anonymised. These are optional.

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

3. Managing cookies

You can control cookies in several ways:

  • Cookie banner: when you first visit, you can accept or reject non-essential cookies.
  • Browser settings: most browsers let you block or delete cookies. See your browser's help pages:
    • Google Chrome
    • Mozilla Firefox
    • Apple Safari
    • Microsoft Edge

Blocking essential cookies may stop parts of the site from working properly.

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page, with the revision date shown above.
