Daily DSE Wrap | Monday, 27 July 2026

VODA Surges 10% on Record Block as NMB Hits New All-Time High

Monday’s session at the Dar es Salaam Stock Exchange was the busiest and most dramatic in weeks. Total equity turnover rocketed to over TZS 14 billion, driven by a wave of enormous institutional block trades. The spotlight fell squarely on VODA, which jumped nearly 10% after a 3.5-million-share block changed hands, and on NMB, which closed at a fresh record high of 16,880. The day provided a powerful lesson in how large investors move money — and how everyday investors can use market rules to their advantage.

Turnover was dominated by five pre-arranged block trades: VODA (3.5 million shares), AFRIPRISE (1 million), MCB (976,000), NICO (250,000), and TBL (260,000). Excluding those giant transactions, normal-board activity remained healthy, with the number of deals nearly doubling to 5,234 — a sign of strong participation from small and mid-sized investors. The All-Share Index was virtually flat, as gains in VODA and NMB were offset by minor declines elsewhere.

Foreign investors were active on both sides of the market. International buying accounted for 18.55% of turnover (TZS 2.67 billion), while foreign selling registered 31.67% (TZS 4.56 billion), likely linked to the large block trades. The one-sided foreign exodus that dominated the first half of 2026 is now a two-way flow — a far healthier environment for the market.
