Daily DSE Wrap | Thursday, 30 July 2026

VODA Rally Peaks as Profit-Takers Cash In and NMB Marches Higher

The Dar es Salaam Stock Exchange climbed for a seventh consecutive session, with the All-Share Index reaching a fresh post-correction high of 4,194.87. The day was dominated by a dramatic intraday spike in VODA to 1,100 — a level that marked the likely peak of its extraordinary post-block-trade rally — followed by a wave of profit-taking that saw the stock close well off its high. NMB continued its relentless advance to yet another record, while CRDB remained in a tight consolidation range. The session provided clear lessons in how markets absorb rapid price moves and why having a disciplined plan matters.
