7. Considerations for a Multi-Year Framework

When a market can take a billion-shilling block trade in its stride and still close the week essentially flat, it has acquired something more valuable than a rally: resilience. The DSE's growing institutional participation — visible in both the equity block board and the record bond session — means prices increasingly reflect considered capital allocation rather than retail sentiment. For the multi-year investor, that is the most constructive backdrop possible.
