Daily DSE Wrap | Friday, 11th September 2026Record Block Supply Absorbed Without a Flinch as Indices Reach New PeaksThe Dar es Salaam Stock Exchange closed the week with a session that demonstrated the remarkable depth of local institutional demand. At the same time, the bond market attracted TZS 19.43 billion in institutional money, proving that both asset classes can draw liquidity simultaneously.1. In Focus: Record Block Absorption and the 5% Daily Price BandFriday's session provided a textbook example of how institutional block trades work in a deep, locally-driven market. Three major blocks changed hands:NMB: 4,904,282 sharesVODA: 600,000 sharesTCC: 114,000 sharesIn total, more than 5.6 million shares — worth approximately TZS 15 billion — were transferred in pre-arranged transactions. Yet the market closed higher. The DSEI rose 35 points. The TSI crossed 10,400.What does this mean for everyday investors?Block trades are negotiated directly between institutions, not through the normal order book. They don't reflect retail demand. But they do signal institutional confidence. When local institutions step forward to absorb millions of shares without demanding a discount, it tells you they believe the stock is worth buying at current prices.The most remarkable aspect of Friday's session was the price action. Despite absorbing 5.6 million shares in blocks, the market did not decline. CRDB rose 2.1%. TCC rose 1.4%. NMB and VODA closed flat. This is what a market looks like when the buyer base is deep and confident.For the daily price band, Friday's closing prices determine Monday's limits.
