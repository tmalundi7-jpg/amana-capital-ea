4. Today’s Concept: Understanding Block Trades vs. Normal Board Orders
In stock exchanges, orders are primarily routed through two execution mechanisms: the **Normal Board** and the **Block Trade Board**.

*   **Normal Board (Continuous Trading):** Orders are matched continuously using a Central Order Book based on Price-Time priority. High-volume market orders placed directly on the normal board can quickly consume bid/offer depth, leading to **slippage** (executing trades at progressively worse prices).
*   **Block Trade Board:** Reserved for transactions exceeding specific regulatory thresholds (typically large block size or monetary value). Buyers and sellers negotiate price and volume off-book via broker-dealers. Once finalized, the trade is printed on the exchange for transparency. This mechanism ensures institutional portfolio rebalancing occurs efficiently without causing panic or price distortion for smaller retail orders on the open market.
