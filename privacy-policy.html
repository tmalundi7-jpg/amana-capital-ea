Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Amana Capital EA Ltd ("Amana Capital EA", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and your rights. Our CMSA (Capital Markets and Securities Authority, Tanzania) registration is currently pending; we operate as an independent research publisher.

1. Data we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address, and the message you send us.
  • Newsletter: your email address, if you subscribe to our research emails.
  • Analytics: anonymised usage data (pages visited, device type, approximate location) collected through standard web analytics tools.

We do not collect payment details, government ID numbers, or any other sensitive personal data through this website.

2. How we use your data

  • To respond to your enquiries.
  • To send you the research emails you subscribed to. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email.
  • To understand how our website is used and improve it.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. How we store your data

Your data is stored securely and accessed only by our team. We keep contact-form records for as long as needed to handle your enquiry, and newsletter subscriptions until you unsubscribe. We apply reasonable technical safeguards against unauthorised access.

4. Your rights

You may at any time:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Ask us to delete your data.
  • Withdraw consent (e.g. unsubscribe from emails).

To exercise any of these rights, email info@amana-capital-ea.co.tz. We will respond within 30 days.

5. Tanzania data protection

We handle personal data in line with the principles of Tanzania's Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 — collecting only what we need, keeping it secure, and respecting your rights over it.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page, with the revision date shown above.

7. Contact us

For any privacy questions: info@amana-capital-ea.co.tz.
