Today, 91% of total equity value on the DSE was concentrated in just two financial institutions: NMB Bank Plc and CRDB Bank Plc. #### Investor Education Takeaway: Navigating Market Depth and Order Discipline

For long-term investors evaluating the market, comparing secondary [bond yields](/investor-education) against equity valuations is critical for balanced asset allocation:

1. **Understand Risk-Adjusted Yields:** Long-dated government paper providing yields between 11.4% and 12.8% sets a risk-free benchmark. Equities must complement this through a mix of high sustainable [dividend yields](/investor-education) and business expansion potential.
2. **Order Book Discipline:** Avoid placing market orders on illiquid or volatile counters. Always analyze the current [order book](/investor-education) depth—checking outstanding [bids](/investor-education) and [offers](/investor-education)—and utilize disciplined limit orders placed around the [volume-weighted average price (VWAP)](/investor-education) to avoid paying unnecessary price premiums.
3. **Respect Daily Price Bands:** Remember that exchange rules impose daily price fluctuation limits (typically ±5% from the prior session close). Submitting order prices outside these operational bands leads to order rejection by broker trading systems. Aligning your orders with prevailing market depth ensures smooth trade execution while maintaining price protection.
