Our Framework

The Four Investment Profiles

Every investor sits on a risk spectrum. Our four profiles are calibrated against real DSE market data, Tanzanian government bond yields, and long-term frontier market return series.

Capital Preservation

Conservative

Safety and liquidity are the primary objectives. Suitable for investors with short time horizons or those who cannot tolerate any meaningful capital loss.

Allocation: 80% Fixed Income · 20% Equities

Income Portfolio

Moderate-Conservative

Steady income with modest capital growth. Favours high-dividend DSE stocks and mid-duration government bonds to deliver predictable cash flows.

Allocation: 55% Fixed Income · 45% Equities

Balanced Growth

Moderate

Diversified exposure to both equity upside and fixed-income stability. Appropriate for investors with a 5–10 year horizon seeking long-run wealth accumulation.

Allocation: 35% Fixed Income · 65% Equities

Aggressive Growth

High Risk

Maximum long-term capital appreciation through concentrated DSE equity exposure. For investors with 10+ year horizons and genuine tolerance for significant short-term drawdowns.

Allocation: 15% Fixed Income · 85% Equities
Available Instruments

What You Can Invest In via the DSE

Amana Capital provides research and advisory coverage across the full spectrum of instruments listed and traded on the Dar es Salaam Stock Exchange and Tanzanian money markets.

📈

DSE Listed Equities

Potential Return: 10–25% p.a.

Shares in companies listed on the Dar es Salaam Stock Exchange, including banking leaders CRDB and NMB, Swissport Tanzania, Tanga Cement, TBL, TPCC, and others. Subject to market liquidity and price volatility.

🏦

Government Treasury Bonds

Yield: ~11–13% p.a.

Fixed-coupon bonds issued by the Government of Tanzania with maturities of 2, 5, 7, 10, 15, and 25 years. Fully backed by sovereign credit. The cornerstone of capital preservation and income portfolios.

💵

Treasury Bills (91/182/364-day)

Yield: ~9–11% p.a.

Short-dated sovereign instruments ideal for cash management and liquidity buffers. Highly liquid, zero credit risk. Suitable for capital preservation allocations or as a parking mechanism between equity deployments.

🏢

Corporate Bonds

Yield: ~12–16% p.a.

Debt instruments issued by DSE-listed corporates and financial institutions. Offer higher yields than government bonds in exchange for credit risk. Require thorough fundamental analysis of issuer financial health.

🌍

East African Cross-Listed Stocks

Varies by market

Shares cross-listed between the DSE, NSE (Kenya), USE (Uganda), and RSE (Rwanda) under the East African Community securities framework. Provides regional diversification within the frontier market spectrum.

💰

Money Market Deposits

Rate: ~8–10% p.a.

High-interest fixed deposits and call accounts at tier-1 Tanzanian commercial banks. Provides TDIC-backed capital protection (up to TZS 1.5m) with liquidity. Appropriate as a core component of conservative portfolios.
